How to publish a manufacturer's article word for word
1. Open the .txt file named after the article (e.g. nibe-launches-next-generation-s2060-heat-pump.txt).
2. Paste the article text under the comment lines (lines starting with # are ignored).
   - Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
   - Start a line with "## " for a sub-heading, "- " for a bullet point.
3. Save, then ask Claude to rebuild. The pasted text replaces our write-up; the hero image,
   product listings table and source link stay.
Only do this where the manufacturer allows republishing (e.g. press releases marked for media use).
